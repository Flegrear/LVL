Storage

Player storage

Space for the videos the player downloads from torrents while you watch. Only the file you play is downloaded, a piece at a time.

  • When you switch to another video, the previous download is deleted.
  • Downloads you aren't watching are deleted after 30 minutes unused, and when the app starts again.
  • If this space fills up, the downloads you used longest ago go first. The video you're watching is never removed.

A video bigger than this limit can't be played until you raise it.

Recommended: 20 GB. Enough for any 1080p movie, or a few episodes at once. 4K movies can be 40 to 80 GB; raise it if you play those. On a nearly full drive the recommendation is at most a quarter of its free space.

Saved video fragments

Short clips of subtitle lines, the audio and a still frame, cut while you watch from the part that already played. Cards use them to replay their line after the video is gone; Dictation uses the rest.

  • A card's clip is kept as long as the card exists.
  • When this space is full, clips only used for dictation go first, least recently used; then the oldest card clips. A card keeps its text, and its line is saved again the next time you watch that episode.

Recommended: 2 GB. That holds about 20,000 lines. The recommendation grows to twice what your cards' clips use, so they always have room.
