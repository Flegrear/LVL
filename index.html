Questions

What is LVL?

A video player, comic reader and media library.

Which devices does it support?

Windows and Android 12 or newer.

Is LVL free?

Yes. LVL's original code uses the MIT license. Third party terms apply.

Does LVL include movies or comic chapters?

No. Add your own files or sources.

Can I open local files?

Yes. Videos and CBZ/ZIP archives. Playback support depends on your device.

Where are my Library and settings kept?

On your device. Online sources connect when used.
